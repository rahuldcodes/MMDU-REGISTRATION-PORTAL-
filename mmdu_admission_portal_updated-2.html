Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

A few notes to help you use this demonstration portal.

Is this the official MMDU admission portal?

No. This is a student-created demonstration webpage. It is not affiliated with or operated by the university and cannot submit an official application.

Does the enquiry form send my details?

No. The form validates your entries in the browser and creates a summary on this page only. Nothing is sent to a server.

How do I apply officially?

Verify current admission steps, eligible courses, deadlines, and contact details through the university's official website or admissions office.

Can I change my selected study area?

Yes. Choose a different option in the form, or use an “Enquire about this area” link in the study area section to preselect it.
